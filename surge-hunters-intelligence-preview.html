What David's community already does

Drivers share job-card screenshots in WhatsApp. Members see current observations and coordinate manually. That existing process is valuable and should not be disrupted without agreement.

What a future system could add

Capture: Screenshot-assisted extraction, voice or taps; driver checks exact pickup point and fare/km.

Organise: A shared dashboard, timestamps, location filters, duplicate handling and stale-report expiry.

Notify: Opt-in WhatsApp or Telegram updates by area, shift and fare threshold, subject to integration permissions and group consent.

Learn: Historical reporting patterns by location, time and fare band, with transparent coverage limitations.

What this demo cannot do

No screenshot OCR, server database, cross-device sharing, push messages, location geocoding, AI forecasting or real-time platform feed. Voice recognition is browser-dependent and may use an external speech service.
