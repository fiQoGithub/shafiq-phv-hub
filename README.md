# SHAFIQ PHV Hub

**DRIVE. LEARN. CREATE. GROW.**

Practical tools, driving-cost calculators, AI learning resources and community knowledge for Singapore's private-hire drivers.

Built from real PHV driving experience, with the goal of helping drivers understand their costs, make informed decisions and develop new digital skills.

## Available tools

- **PHV Take-Home Tracker** — Record driving income and expenses to estimate take-home earnings.
- **EV Calculator** — Explore electric vehicle operating costs.
- **Knowledge Centre** — Access useful driving and learning resources.
- **Creator Showcase** — Discover community contributors and creators.
- **Feedback** — Share suggestions to improve the Hub.

## Fuel Optimiser — v0.8 Beta

A petrol and hybrid fuel-cost comparison tool under development.

The beta aims to support different PHV platforms, driver tiers, payment methods, fuel memberships, discounts and detour costs.

**Important:** Fuel prices and promotional benefits may change. Unverified offers and illustrative calculations should not be treated as guaranteed savings.
